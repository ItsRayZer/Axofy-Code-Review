Your keys are stored only on this computer (in the app's user data folder). They are never uploaded, logged, or put in code. Leave a field blank to keep the key already saved.

How free works: the review runs on your computer. The AI call goes to the free tier of the provider you pick — NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter or Gemini pay for that compute as a free tier. If the free tier is busy, reviews queue or fall back to the next free provider. Bringing your own free key (BYOK) means Axofy never pays for your reviews, and you never pay Axofy.
